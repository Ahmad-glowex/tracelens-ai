Privacy & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Zero Telemetry

At TraceLens AI, operated by Raihan LLC, we are developers building for developers. We recognize that error logs and stack traces often contain sensitive directory names, environment variables, or proprietary logic. Our architectural philosophy is built on client-side sandboxing and zero telemetry of user code snippets.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Source Code & Stacks: We do not log, retain, or store your inputted terminal crashes or generated Git patches on centralized databases.
  • Your API Credentials: Keys provided in Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) mode are stored exclusively inside your web browser's isolated localStorage.
  • Model Training: Your input data is never sold, shared with data brokers, or utilized to fine-tune AI foundation models.

3. Direct Client-to-API Communication

When executing queries via Groq or Google Gemini API, requests are transmitted over encrypted TLS directly to the inference endpoint specified by your client configuration. No intermediary proxy inspects your payload in BYOK mode.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site tracking cookies. We utilize browser local storage solely to retain your client preferences (such as custom API tokens and preferred runtime tabs).

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we do not store personal identifying code logs on our servers, there is no persistent repository of your technical diagnostics to request deletion for. You may clear your browser cache and local storage at any time to remove all client-side state.
